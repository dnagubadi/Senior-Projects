Each of these projects does one part of what Spark does. None of them does all of it.

| Project | What it is | Like Spark | Different from Spark |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Project Genie** (Google DeepMind, 2026) | You describe a world, AI generates it, and you explore it in real time. | Works for any subject | Screen only |
| **Dataland** (Refik Anadol, Los Angeles, 2026) | An AI art museum filled with immersive generated visuals. | AI and immersive | You only watch |
| **Exploratorium** (San Francisco) | Hands-on science exhibits designed to make visitors curious. | Physical play | One fixed topic |

# The gap Spark fills

Spark puts the three together: any subject, like Project Genie; an immersive, AI-made experience, like Dataland; and hands-on physical play, like the Exploratorium. It also adds something none of them has, which is a way to take your Spark home and keep going.
