> **Example draft.** This shows how a journey works. The details will change after playtesting.

A journey is a game template that the AI fills in for any subject. This one is called **The Expedition**. You travel through three places, each one a little deeper into the subject, find things along the way, and make something new at the end.

# The template

The AI fills in each slot for your subject. Here's the same journey filled in two ways.

| Slot | What the AI fills in | Volcano Spark | Ocean Spark |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **World** | A place to explore | An island with a sleeping volcano | A coral reef that drops into the deep sea |
| **Guide** | A friendly character who asks questions instead of giving lectures | Ember, a lava lizard | Pip, a curious octopus |
| **Goal** | One thing to work toward | Wake the volcano and build a new island | Meet a creature from every depth |
| **Three stops** | Places that go deeper each time | Beach, lava tubes, crater | Reef, twilight zone, deep sea floor |
| **Discoveries** | Small surprises you find by exploring | Kinds of rock, steam vents, lava flows | Fish, glowing jellies, anglerfish |
| **Ignite** | What pressing Ignite makes | Erupts a new island | Makes a new creature |

# How it plays

1. **Arrive.** The guide says hi and asks one question, like "What do you think is under this island?" There's no wrong answer.
2. **Explore the first stop.** Move with the joystick. Anything you bump into tells you one short, surprising thing.
3. **Turn the knobs.** Each knob changes the world, and you see what happens right away.
4. **Press Ignite.** Make something new from what you've found.
5. **Go deeper.** Steps 2 to 4 repeat at the next two stops. Each stop opens up one more knob or button.
6. **Finish and keep it.** Your world, your discoveries and everything you made are saved to My Sparks. You can open it later in Spark Studio and keep building.

# Sparkplug controls in the Volcano Spark

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Joystick | Walk around the island |
| Knob 1 | Heat: from cool rock to glowing lava |
| Knob 2 | Pressure: how close the volcano is to erupting |
| Knob 3 | Time: watch years pass in seconds |
| Button 1 | Look closer at what's in front of you |
| Button 2 | Ask Ember a question |
| Button 3 | Collect a discovery |
| Button 4 | Open the map |
| Ignite | Erupt and build a new island |

# Why it feels safe to try

Spark is for people who hold back from things they're curious about, so this journey is built so there's nothing to lose:

- **No way to fail.** There's no score, no timer and no game over.
- **Every action does something.** Turning a knob or pressing a button always changes the world, so you learn by trying.
- **The guide asks, it doesn't test.** Questions are invitations, not quizzes.
- **You leave with something you made,** not a grade.
