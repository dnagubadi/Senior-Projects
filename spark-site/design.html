Colors

The orange half and the blue half come straight from the Spark hemisphere. Cream, peach, sky and sun support them.

Contrast rule: bright orange and bright blue never carry text on light backgrounds. Text uses navy, ember, slate or blue 600. Contrast ratios are listed next to every pair in tokens.css.

Type

Bricolage Grotesque for headings and buttons: friendly, with a bit of personality. Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for reading: designed so every letter is easy to tell apart.

How Sparkplug works

Lead · 20–24px

A bigger paragraph that introduces a section in one or two sentences.

Body · 18–20px

Body text is at least 18px so it's comfortable for young readers. Lines stay under about 65 characters wide, so eyes don't get lost.

Small · 15–16px

Captions, hints and tags. Never smaller than this.

Links

Links are always underlined, so you can spot them without relying on color. They get a thicker line on hover.

Buttons

Buttons are pill shaped and at least 48px tall, so they're easy to tap. Each kind looks different even without color: filled, outlined or underlined.

On light

On navy

  • Hover: lifts up 2px. Primary gets lighter.
  • Pressed: pushes down onto its navy edge.
  • Keyboard focus: thick blue ring outside the button.
  • Disabled: grey fill, dashed edge and a "not allowed" cursor.

Cards

Shape and space

Spacing grows on a 4px base. Corners are round and friendly. Shadows are soft and tinted navy.

Screen sizes

Layouts start as a single column on phones, then spread out: two columns from 640px, three from 1024px. The docs table of contents becomes a sticky sidebar from 960px.
