# Hero image

`index.html` expects four files here:

- `ireland-hero-960.avif` and `ireland-hero-960.webp` (mobile)
- `ireland-hero-1920.avif` and `ireland-hero-1920.webp` (desktop)

Use one still Irish landscape (coastal grassland, cliffs, hills), roughly 3:2, with the focal point on the right third so it stays clear of the text. Keep the 960px AVIF under about 250 KB (120 to 180 KB is realistic). Squoosh (squoosh.app) exports both formats.

Until the files exist the hero shows the peat background colour, and every piece of text still meets contrast.
